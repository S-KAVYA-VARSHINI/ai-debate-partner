
DEBATE TOPIC: AI IN EDUCATION

AI can personalize learning by adapting content difficulty,
providing immediate feedback, and allowing students to practice
at their own pace.

However, AI-based education also has limitations. Automated
systems may have difficulty understanding emotional, social,
and contextual factors affecting a student's learning.

Human teachers can provide mentorship, emotional support,
classroom management, social interaction, and contextual
judgment that automated systems may not fully reproduce.

AI can therefore be considered a support tool for education,
while the role of teachers can include supervision, mentorship,
and human interaction.

DEBATE TOPIC: SOCIAL MEDIA

Social media can improve communication, information sharing,
community building, and access to educational content.

However, excessive social media use can contribute to distraction,
reduced productivity, misinformation, and problematic patterns
of use.

The effects of social media can therefore depend on how,
why, and how frequently it is used.

DEBATE TOPIC: ONLINE EDUCATION

Online education provides flexibility, remote access to learning,
and the ability to study from different locations.

However, online learning can involve challenges such as reduced
face-to-face interaction, distractions, unequal access to
technology, and difficulties maintaining motivation.

DEBATE TOPIC: CLIMATE CHANGE

Human activities, particularly greenhouse gas emissions from
burning fossil fuels, are a major driver of observed modern
climate change.

Reducing emissions can involve renewable energy, energy
efficiency, changes in transportation, and other mitigation
strategies.

Climate policy can involve trade-offs involving economic costs,
energy security, technological development, and environmental
benefits.
